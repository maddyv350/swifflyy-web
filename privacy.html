Privacy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

Swifflyy hasn’t launched yet. Right now, this website does exactly one thing with your data: if you join the early-access list, we store the email address (and, optionally, the city) you give us, so we can email you when Swifflyy goes live near you.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details you submit — your email and optional city.
  • Referral attribution — if you arrived through a friend’s link, we record their referral code alongside your signup so they get credit.
  • Basic analytics — we use Google Tag Manager to understand aggregate traffic (pages viewed, rough location, device type). No advertising profiles are built by us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your email to anyone.
  • We don’t send marketing beyond launch updates for Swifflyy.
  • This website never asks for, or accesses, your device’s GPS location.

Removal

Want off the list? Email support@swifflyy.com from the address you signed up with and we’ll delete it.

When the app launches, it will ship with its own full privacy policy covering location, profiles and verification — written before a single profile goes live.
