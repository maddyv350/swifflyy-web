Content Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Swifflyy is for real adults meeting real people nearby. This policy is part of our Terms of Use and applies to everything on the app: photos, prompt answers, bios, messages and how you behave towards other users.

Zero tolerance

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. Accounts that break these rules are removed, sometimes without warning, and we cooperate with law enforcement where the law requires it.

Not allowed

  • Nudity and sexual content - explicit or suggestive photos, sexual solicitation, or unsolicited sexual messages.
  • Harassment and hate - threats, bullying, stalking, slurs, or discrimination based on race, religion, caste, gender, sexuality, disability or anything else.
  • Anything involving minors - Swifflyy is 18+ only. Any sexualisation of a minor is reported to the authorities and results in a permanent ban. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • Fake profiles and impersonation - pretending to be someone else, using photos that are not of you, or misrepresenting your age.
  • Spam, scams and solicitation - commercial promotion, asking for money, gifts or financial details, links to other platforms for the purpose of soliciting, or repetitive unsolicited messages.
  • Violence and illegal activity - threats or glorification of violence, promotion of self-harm, illegal drugs, weapons, or any other unlawful activity.
  • Privacy violations - sharing another person's private information, photos, screenshots or messages without their consent.

Your profile and photos

  • Use recent photos of yourself that clearly show your face. At least your first photo must be you, alone.
  • No nudity or underwear shots, no children on their own, no weapons, no watermarks, contact details or promotional text.
  • Prompt answers and bios follow the same rules as photos.
  • The verified ✓ badge means a selfie matched your profile photos; using someone else's photos to get it is grounds for a ban.

Report and block

  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, open their profile or chat and tap “Report, block & unmatch”.
  • Blocking is immediate: the match is removed and that person can no longer reach you or appear in your deck.
  • Every report is reviewed by our team, normally within 24 hours. When a chat is reported we may read that conversation to investigate. Reports are confidential - the other person is not told who reported them.
  • Anything urgent, or anything involving a minor: support@swifflyy.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the police first.

Enforcement

  • Depending on severity we warn, remove content, suspend, or permanently ban the account. Serious violations skip straight to a ban.
  • Bans apply to the person, not just the profile. Creating a new account to get around a ban is itself a violation.
  • We keep a record of reports, blocks and enforcement so repeat behaviour can be acted on, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • To appeal a decision, email support@swifflyy.com from your registered details. We reply within 1–2 business days.
